This sheet follows set C. No owner screenshot shows this room, so the next rule applies: it is on screen longest (45 s against 14 and 11), it is the set the room is named after, and the map already takes its wanted case, vending machine and floor grates from it. Set A is listed second because the map's alarm arrival (the scout at 1:02) happens there.

The set's true span: 1:21.5 to 2:13.0. The brief's 0:50 to 2:00 starts on set A and stops 13 s early.

Set C is about 7.7 H (14 m) wide along the back wall and the fight uses a floor about 2.5 H (4.5 m) deep. The camera never shows the right side wall or the ceiling.

## What the newest map render has (build zu)

Game camera and film-fitted camera renders of the room:

- A flat back wall with a single low red skirting line; no panel frames, no recesses, no jutting block.
- Three small wanted frames at about 0.18, 0.45 and 0.70 across, each hung on a rod from a rail that runs the width of the room between two black corner posts. The film has one large case fixed to the wall and no rail.
- Two dark doorways in the back wall at about 0.33 and 0.72, each with a white light bar over it.
- A vending rack between them at about 0.55, roughly 0.8 H tall, with a small row of items beside it.
- Four flat floor grates (three near the back wall, one near the front). No pit, no rim, no lid.
- Two or three door frames with red-topped posts along each side wall.
- A red line across the floor near the front, floor otherwise one flat slab.
- A "reward" sign low on the back wall.

## Gaps, ranked by what a viewer of the film would miss

1. The red-framed wall panelling: the 5.6 m bordered panel behind the case and at least one recessed bay (1.6 x 2.0 m, hazard sill 0.45 m up, at 0.57 across). This is what makes the hall read as the film's; the map wall is plain.
2. One big wanted case fixed to the wall at 0.24 across (1.3 x 1.6 m, bottom 0.5 m up, conduit to the ceiling) instead of three small frames hanging from a rail the film does not have.
3. The floor hatch: an open pit 2.3 x 1.2 m with a 0.25 m rim and its lid lying beside it, 2 m out from the wall at 0.27 across, in place of flat grates. The only thing in the set that breaks the floor.
4. The vending machine and the door beside it: film has the machine at 0.73 across (1.6 wide, 2.4 tall, 0.7 deep, angled red housing) with the one back doorway right of it at 0.88 (1.2 x 2.2 m). The map has a shorter rack at centre between two doorways.
5. The jutting wall block with the red edge strip at 0.48 across (0.3 m wide and proud, full height), the one piece of cover the film uses, and the single left side door against the map's rows of side doors with posts.

Smaller: the corner posts, the ceiling rail and the door light bars are not in the film.
