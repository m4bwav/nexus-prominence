# Room 5, Sniper loft: film structure sheet

Study date 2026-11-15. Source: the film NEXUS: HANK RAID, frames pulled at 2 per second for 8:25 to 9:50 and 5:45 to 6:50, plus the owner's screenshots and the build zu renders (game camera and film camera).

A grunt is taken as 1.8 m. "h" below means one character height. Across-room positions run 0 (left wall) to 1 (right wall) as the camera sees the room. All sizes are read from stills drawn in loose perspective, so treat metres as plus or minus 20 percent unless marked high.

## Which film set this room follows

The brief guessed that the film at 8:40 to 9:35 is the sniper loft. It is not. That stretch is one long hall with a pillar at its left end and the pylons and generator at its right end; the camera pans from one end to the other at 8:59.5 to 9:00.5 without a cut. It belongs with the garage (see the room 6 sheet).

The sniper loft is the set at **5:51.5 to 6:19.5**. It is the only place in the film with a sniper on a balcony and a ladder, and the owner's `sniper-balcony-ladder` screenshot is from it (6:03.0 to 6:06.5, a held shot). The set opens with a tilted close shot of the entry door (5:51.5 to 5:57.0), turns level at 5:57.5, and ends on a fade through the left door at 6:19.5. The next shot (6:20.0) is the shutter room.

## Gaps, ranked by what a viewer of the film would miss

1. **The sloped low wall** (4 m long, 1.3 m tall, diagonal, from 0.50 at the wall to 0.62 about 4 m out). It is in most of the set's wide shots and is the enemy's main cover. The map has nothing there.
2. **The balcony as a thick slab at 2 h** (4.5 x 1.2 x 0.6 m, top at about 3.6 m, chamfered underside, no rail, 0.62 to 0.88). The map's thin railed shelf at 1.2 h is the weakest match to the owner's screenshot.
3. **Ladder at the right end with the pier beside it** (ladder at 0.88, 0.85 m wide; pier at 0.93, 0.9 m wide, 0.5 m proud), and the red band rising in an upright stripe to deck level. The map's ladder is at the wrong end and the wall is flat.
4. **Crates at film size**: the big braced crate at 0.20 should be about 2.6 m wide and 2.4 m tall, solid enough to hide behind; add the stacked pair (2.1 m with 1.0 m on top) at 0.72 under the balcony.
5. **Doors as the film draws them**: the doorway on the deck behind the sniper (0.70, 1.6 x 2.1 m), the back-wall emblem door at 0.42 in a proud frame, and one arrival door in the right wall about 2 m from the back corner. The map has plain frames and a chevron panel.

Smaller: the second right-wall pier; the film room is darker and greyer than the other rooms, with almost no props on the floor.

## Notes for whoever builds this

- The film's balcony is higher than a jump. If the game cannot path a sniper up 3.6 m, keep the deck where the sniper points work and take the look from the film: slab thickness, chamfer, no rail, ladder at the right end.
- The sloped wall changes sight lines from the entry door to the right half of the room; it belongs behind its own toggle.
- The pillar, the sloped blocks and the striped steps in the owner's 8:49 screenshot are not this room. They are in the room 6 sheet.
