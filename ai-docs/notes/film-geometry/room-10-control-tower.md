# Room 10, Control tower: film structure sheet

Study date 2026-11-15. Source: the film NEXUS: HANK RAID, frames pulled at 2 per second for 9:20 to 11:15 (wider than the brief's 10:10 to 11:15), contact sheets at 1 per second, the owner's admin-room screenshot, and the build zu renders (game camera and film camera).

A grunt is taken as 1.8 m; "h" is one character height. Across-room positions run 0 (left wall) to 1 (right wall) as the camera sees the room. Sizes come from stills in loose perspective; treat metres as plus or minus 20 percent.

## Which film set this room follows

**The admin room, 10:34.0 to about 11:01.0 (27 s).** It is the film's last fight set: a box room with both side walls in view, a sign reading ADMIN ROOM over a right-side doorway, two screens hung from ceiling mounts on the back wall, a tall red light panel. The owner's screenshot `owner-2026-11-15-admin-room.png` is this set (it matches the 10:47 frame line for line), so the brief's first guess (room 2 or 3) is wrong and the screenshot belongs here. The README calls the room a control tower; the film's last room is an admin office, not a tower with a wall of monitors, so the set matches the README's place in the route (the last room, the last stand) but not its name or its monitor wall.

- 10:34.0 to 10:51.5: wide shots, the fight in the room.
- 10:52.0 to 10:53.5: black (cut).
- 10:54.0 to 11:01.0: close shots in the same room (console, bodies, two late enemies).
- 11:01.5 to 11:05.5: the last beat through a door marked NP (an elevator or exit), not a fight set.
- 11:06 on: white flash, then the title and credits.
- Before 10:33.5 the film is in the canteen (room 9's set).

## Notes for whoever builds this

- The film room is small (about 15 x 6 m). If the map room stays larger, keep the left-to-right order (panel, console, screens, red light, signed door) on the back and right walls and leave the extra floor bare.
- The README's monitor wall could stay behind a toggle as the "control tower" idea, but it is not in the film.
- Only the bench and console change cover; the rest is wall and overhead dressing.
