# Room 6, Shutter garage: film structure sheet

Study date 2026-11-15. Source: the film NEXUS: HANK RAID, frames pulled at 2 per second for 5:45 to 6:50 and 8:25 to 9:50, plus the owner's screenshots and the build zu renders (game camera and film camera).

A grunt is taken as 1.8 m. "h" below means one character height. Across-room positions run 0 (left wall) to 1 (right wall) as the camera sees the room. Sizes come from stills drawn in loose perspective; treat metres as plus or minus 20 percent unless marked high.

## Which film set this room follows

The film shows two sets that the map's garage draws on.

- **Set A, the pillar and pylon hall, 8:42.0 to 9:37.5 (55 s).** One long room. Its left half (8:42.0 to 8:59.5) has the pillar, the low barricades and the tall sloped blocks; the camera then pans right without a cut (8:59.5 to 9:00.5) to the stepped platform, the two pylons, the hose coil and the generator (9:00.5 to 9:37.5). It ends on a fade through the right-wall door at 9:37.5. Three of the owner's screenshots are from this set: `0849-pillar-and-sloped-blocks` at 8:49.0, `garage-wide-floor-edges` at about 9:02.0, and `garage-pylons-generator` at about 9:13.0.
- **Set B, the shutter room, 6:20.0 to 6:39.0 (19 s).** A smaller, red-lit room with the roller shutter and the console bank. It gives the room its name. None of the owner's screenshots are from it.

By the brief's rule (the set in the owner's screenshots first, then the one on screen longest) the room follows **set A**, and keeps set B's shutter and console on the back wall, which the map already has. The brief's first reading put the 8:49 screenshot in room 5; the pan at 9:00 shows it is the same room as the two garage screenshots.

Before 8:42 the film is in a different room (posters, a table, a box on a stand, 8:34 to 8:41). After 9:37.5 it is a red room with barrels and notices.

The map's are both about 1.8 m, far apart and left of centre. Both owner garage screenshots are framed on this pair.
2. **The stepped platform under them.** Upper tier about 0.45 m high (0.40 to 0.60 across, 0 to 2 m out), lower tier about 0.2 m (0.40 to 0.68, 2 to 5 m out), red riser between the tiers, red-striped steps 0.9 m wide on the left flank. 3. **The tall sloped blocks.** Wedge walls 2.2 m tall, 0.9 m wide, red top edge, emblem on the face: two at the platform's left corners (0.40 across, 1 m and 4 m out), two more near 0.50 and 0.60 at about 6 m out.

## Notes for whoever builds this

- Set A is about 22 m across and the map room may be narrower. If so, keep the order left to right (pillar line, sloped blocks and steps, platform with pylons, hose, generator, sign, jog) and squeeze the gaps, not the pieces. The shutter and console can sit on the back wall between the pillar and the platform, where set A's wall is bare.
- If the pillar half will not fit, it is the part to move: it could stand in another room, but the film is clear that it shares a room with the pylons.
- The near pylon is used as a perch at 9:23 to 9:24. The platform, the steps and the blocks change cover and routes, so each belongs behind its own toggle.
- Whether the game can walk a 0.2 m and a 0.45 m step is not known from the film study; a ramp behind the striped treads would look the same from the game camera.
