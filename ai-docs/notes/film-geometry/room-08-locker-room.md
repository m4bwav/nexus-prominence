# Room 8, Locker room: film structure sheet

Study date 2026-11-15. Source: the film NEXUS: HANK RAID, frames pulled at 2 per second for 7:40 to 8:55 (the brief's 8:00 to 8:35 widened by 20 s each side), plus the build zu renders (game camera and film camera).

A grunt is taken as 1.8 m. "h" means one character height. Across-room positions run 0 (left) to 1 (right) as the camera sees the set. Sizes come from stills drawn in loose perspective; treat metres as plus or minus 20 percent.

## Which film set this room follows

- **True span: 7:59.5 to 8:31.5 (32 s).** It matches the README room: lockers, emblem panels, two red spotlights from the ceiling. The set is one long room seen in two halves: the locker half (7:59.5 to 8:11.5, with a white flash at 8:07.5) and, after a cut, the right half with a cabinet, a crate, a wall box on a pipe and a flight of steps (8:12.0 to 8:31.5). The locker wall shows again behind the fight at 8:21 to 8:28, which ties the two halves into one room.
- Before it (7:43.0 to 7:58.5) is a different, red-tinted set with barrels, crates, a long shelf and emblem doors; it is not the locker room. After it (8:32.0 to 8:41.0) is a third set with a wanted poster, tables and a box on a stand; then black, and the pillar hall from 8:42.
- The brief's 8:00 to 8:35 is close; the true end is 3.5 s earlier.

2. **The steps in the right half.** Five or six treads, about 1.4 m wide and 2.7 m long, rising 0.45 m to a landing, at 0.55 to 0.95 across, 1 to 4 m out; the room's only change of floor level and the shield guard's spot.
3. **The emblem door in the back wall.** 1.8 x 2.4 m at 0.40 to 0.55 across, beside the lockers; it opens for a grunt. The render has a red frame and an arrow sign there.
4. **The cabinet and crate cover pair.** A 1.6 m tall, 1.4 m wide cabinet at 0.70 across, 0.5 to 1.5 m out, and a 0.9 m crate beside it at 0.77; the right half's cover.

## Notes for whoever builds this
