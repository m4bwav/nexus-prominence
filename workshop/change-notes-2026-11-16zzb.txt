Five more rooms rebuilt to match the film. Every new piece is solid cover: shots stop at it and you walk round it.

- Street checkpoint: the window now sits behind a waist-high sill with wall each side and a dark pilaster at its right end, as in the film's opening shot. The hung sign moved left of centre and the folded boom barrier is gone.
- Cargo bay: one strapped crate alone in the middle of the floor to fight round, and a low raised deck along the back wall with a sloped end (solid, not climbable yet). The clutter along the back wall is gone.
- Sniper loft: a big braced crate by the way in, a long sloped low wall the guards hold, stacked crates and a wall pier. The balcony is now a thick slab with its ladder at the right end.
- Locker room: three tall lockers and a column, a free-standing cabinet and crate to fight round, a solid flight of steps on the right, an emblem door, and two red spotlight cones you can shoot out.
- Control tower: rebuilt as the film's admin room: a hooded console, a low bench and a tower unit to fight round, two hanging screens that still break when shot, a red light panel and a lit sign over the side door. The monitor wall and server racks are gone.
