Playground map: Hank storms the Nexus Prominence control tower, ten rooms chained left to right from the street checkpoint to the control tower (wanted hall, controls office, cargo bay, sniper loft, shutter garage, spotlight corridor, locker room, break room). Nexus agents, guards, scouts, engineers and soldats get more alert the deeper you go.

[h2]The rooms[/h2]
Every room is a different fight, staged from the film: an ambush on two guards with their backs turned, a hall where one more walks in, an office behind desks, a shield wall in the cargo bay, a sniper on the loft balcony, a quiet garage, a gauntlet down the corridor, close quarters in the lockers, a break room caught off guard and a last stand in the tower. Every room has a Nexus weapon lying near the entrance, and ammo and med cabinets wait before the hard rooms. Shoot the set up as you go: the wanted hall's display case shatters, the spotlight corridor's lamps go dark and the control tower's monitor wall cracks screen by screen.

Based on the fan collab "NEXUS: HANK RAID" (watch it: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v0suvMY3GgM). No art from the video is used. The rooms are built from simple shapes drawn with the game's own outlined terrain shader, in the film's gray-and-red look. Each room has its own light and one landmark, and the larger props (crates, counters, desk, lockers, a cabinet) are modelled in code instead of left as plain boxes. Props that look solid block movement.

How it was made: the map is generated by a script, and an AI coding agent (Claude) wrote and tuned that script under the uploader's direction. The tiling wall and floor textures were made with a local image model (Z-Image, Apache-2.0) and then tinted. Every room was checked against frames from the film in editor renders.

Use: enable the mod from the gear icon, set the difficulty at the Playground terminal in the Default Chamber, then load Nexus Prominence from it. The map has no Playground control room of its own, so there is no spawn menu inside; leave through the pause menu.

Reinforcements follow the film. Backup walks in through the side doors of the wanted hall, cargo bay, spotlight corridor and control tower a few seconds after the guards spot you, while the fight is still on (in the hall only if the alarm goes up). Clearing a room and walking back no longer meets anyone new. The other rooms keep their doors shut. No room holds more enemies, backup included, than the film shows (2, about 4, 3, 7, 4, 3, 5, 3, 4, 5). Timed spike strips sit in the shutter garage and spotlight corridor.

Not yet confirmed in play: how the staged fights play (who holds cover, the sniper, the walk-in), when backup arrives, how the textures, modelled props and the map's own walls look at game resolution. Early test build: tell us in the comments about doors that don't connect, rooms you can't clear, walls in odd places, or backup that never comes or comes too early.
